Anterior (80%) ICA Inlet → splits straight out to MCA (lateral) & up/medially to ACA.
Posterior (20%) L + R Vertebral → merge into Basilar (Brainstem/Cerebellum) → split into L + R PCA.
Circle of Willis AComA (L↔R ACA) + PComA (ICA↔PCA) act as emergency bypass bridges.

Normal Cerebral Perfusion

  • 80% of blood enters via the two Internal Carotid Arteries (ICA), immediately feeding MCA and ACA.
  • 20% of blood enters from the bottom via Vertebral Arteries, merges into the Basilar Artery, supplies Brainstem/Cerebellum, and exits via PCA.
  • Click any artery or press Embolus → L MCA to see why clots love the MCA and what happens downstream.
